Rota Wrapped

A dashboard of rota cover, listener contribution and leader cover, built from your branch's Three Rings data at the click of a button.

Your data stays with you

This website holds code only. When you click the button inside Three Rings, your own browser fetches the reports using your own login, so you only ever see what your Three Rings permissions allow. The figures are drawn on your screen and disappear when you close the tab. Nothing is stored, uploaded or sent to this website or anyone else.

Add the buttons

  1. Show your bookmarks bar: press Ctrl+Shift+B (on a Mac, Cmd+Shift+B).
  2. Drag a button onto the bookmarks bar. Clicking it here does nothing.

Using it

  1. Log in to Three Rings as normal.
  2. Click the button on your bookmarks bar. A new tab opens and your report appears after a few seconds.
  3. If your browser says it blocked a pop-up, allow pop-ups for www.3r.org.uk and click the button again.

  2. In Safari, bookmark any page (tap the share icon, then Add Bookmark) and give it the button's name.
  3. Open your bookmarks, tap Edit, tap that bookmark, delete its address and paste the code in its place. Tap Done.
  4. To use it: log in to Three Rings in Safari, then open your bookmarks and tap it.

  2. In Chrome, bookmark any page (tap the star), then edit the bookmark: give it the button's name, delete its address and paste the code in its place.
